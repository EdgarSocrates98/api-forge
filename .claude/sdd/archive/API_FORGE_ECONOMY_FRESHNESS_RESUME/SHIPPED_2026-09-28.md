## Lessons Learned

### Technical

- Reserve safety floors before splitting a budget, or the floors silently overspend it.
- A freshness window must say what it measures from; receipt age and pack age answer different questions.
- Code-emission checks read literals; keep catalogued codes out of f-string prefixes.

### Process

- A final gap-closing wave works best when each gap maps to one verb and one eval kind.
